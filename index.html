How to play

Tap the keys at the bottom. Hold a key to grow a long note. Slide your finger across the keys for a glissando. Use lots of fingers at once.

  • Octave − / + moves the whole keyboard lower or higher.
  • Keys switches between one, two, three and four octaves.
  • Colours changes the glow. Notes gives every note name its own colour.
  • Sound picks the instrument. Sustain lets notes ring on after you let go.
  • Effect picks the particle style and Sparkle sets how many fly. Letters shows note names on the keys.
  • 🎬 in My recordings turns a recording into a video with sound. It replays in real time, so keep the screen open, then tap Save video to save it to Photos or Files.
  • ● Record captures everything you play. ▶ Play back plays the last recording with the notes falling onto the keys, and My recordings keeps them all. Tap a name to rename it.
  • ▲ hides the toolbar so the whole screen is piano. Tap ▼ menu to bring it back.

Full screen: tap ⤢ Full screen in the toolbar. For the best version on an iPad, in Safari tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen like an app every time, with its own icon.

On a computer keyboard: A S D F G H J K L are white keys, W E T Y U O P are black keys, Z / X change octave.
