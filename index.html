How to play

Tap the keys at the bottom. Hold a key to grow a long note. Slide your finger across the keys for a glissando. Use lots of fingers at once.

  • − / + moves the whole keyboard an octave lower or higher.
  • ⚙ Settings has everything else: how many octaves, the colours, the background scene, the particle effect and how much sparkle, the instrument sound, sustain and the letters on the keys.
  • ♪ Tune plays a built-in tune with the notes falling onto the keys. Tap again for the next tune.
  • 🎬 in the 🎞 recordings list turns a recording into a video with sound. It replays in real time, so keep the screen open, then tap Save video to save it to Photos or Files.
  • ● Record captures everything you play. ▶ Play back plays the last recording with the notes falling onto the keys, and 🎞 lists them all. Tap a name to rename it.
  • ▲ hides the toolbar so the whole screen is piano. Tap ▼ menu to bring it back.

Full screen: tap ⤢ in the toolbar. For the best version on an iPad, in Safari tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen like an app every time, with its own icon.

On a computer keyboard: A S D F G H J K L are white keys, W E T Y U O P are black keys, Z / X change octave.
